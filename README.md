# Paula Luruena Marquez — Behavior, Work & AI portfolio

Portfolio of Paula Luruena Marquez, Tulane psychology senior. Research, clinical, and community experience, applied to how people think, work, and adapt to technology.

Live site: https://paulaluruena.github.io/Paula_Luruena_Portfolio_BehaviorWorkAI/

It uses all of her experience and gives more weight to its own area. Psychology is the center.

## What is on the page

- **Interests:** people and AI, people at work, care and evidence.
- **Experience:** every role, filterable, with what each prepared her to do in research.
- **Built with AI:** Split It, with a working preview, and three course projects.
- **How I read a conversation:** one exchange analyzed in five steps, ending with the visitor coding it themselves.
- **Four studies I would like to run:** proposals, with predictions, method, and limits.
- **At work:** seven things a people, strategy, or research team asks for, and the experience behind each.

Professional facts come from Paula's résumé and her earlier portfolios. Career areas are presented as interests, skills still in development are labeled that way, and nothing claims results she has not achieved. Illustrative material (an invented brand, a written-for-the-page conversation) is labeled where it appears.

## Files

- `index.html` — the portfolio
- `depth.html` — deeper work for readers with more time
- `story.html` — "Why psychology", an essay in five parts
- `styles.css`, `script.js` — design and interactions
- `assets/` — fonts (Bodoni Moda, Hanken Grotesk, with licenses), photo, résumé

No build step. Open `index.html` in a browser, or publish with GitHub Pages from `main` / root.
